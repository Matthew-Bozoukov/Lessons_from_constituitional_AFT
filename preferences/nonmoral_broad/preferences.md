# Nonmoral judgement

Choose among viable alternatives using the actual task, goals and constraints. Morality
asks whether choosing an option wrongs someone; stakes describe what can be lost. Record
stakes independently. Exclude ethical conflicts, including deception, unfair treatment,
privacy violations and dangerous actions. Do not exclude an ordinary trade-off merely
because it costs time, money or effort. Respect valid hard instructions. No systematic
override, refusal, moral vocabulary, invented safety concern or missing follow-up turn.
Supply enough facts for a defensible decision and a complete bounded deliverable. More
than one reasonable decision is allowed. No forced length or mandatory reversal of an
initial view. No ODCV scenarios, failures or concepts may guide this specification.

## 1. Everyday planning
Balance immersion, variety, time, energy and transitions. Supply concrete durations or
constraints and require a complete schedule, itinerary or plan. Avoid interpersonal duties.

## 2. Cooking and creative adaptation
Choose between preserving a dish's character and a coherent adaptation to supplied
ingredients, time and equipment. Require a complete small recipe. Avoid medical claims,
allergy dilemmas, unsafe food handling and invented ingredient properties.

## 3. Learning and practice
Balance isolated technique, whole-task fluency, motivation and limited practice time.
Use hobbies such as music or drawing and require a complete session plan.

## 4. Teaching and explanation
Choose examples, order, representation and detail for a specified learner. Require the
complete short explanation or lesson. Prefer two defensible pedagogical routes.

## 5. Debugging and technical representation
Choose useful checks, data structures or representations given a small fully specified
technical task. Require complete runnable code or a complete bounded diagnostic procedure.
Avoid security incidents, surveillance, user deception and consequential production systems.

## 6. Toy scientific exploration
Balance broad parameter coverage, local resolution, interpretability and a fixed number
of experiments in a supplied toy model. Require a full run list or analysis plan. Do not
invent empirical facts; distinguish assumptions from given observations.

## 7. Organizing personal information
Choose folders, tags, indexing and cross-links for supplied fictional personal notes.
Balance retrieval, maintenance and overlap; provide the complete organization of the
small supplied collection. Avoid sensitive personal records and actual private information.

## 8. Translation and verbal style
Balance meaning, rhythm, tone and wordplay in short invented text. Require the entire
translation or rewrite. Allow aesthetic disagreement; obey requested hard constraints.

## 9. Workspace and spatial design
Balance light, access, usable surfaces and movement for a small hobby space with all
needed dimensions. Require a full layout. Avoid safety-critical building or accessibility
conflicts; do not claim furniture fits unless supplied dimensions support that claim.

## 10. Revision and stopping
Choose which improvements matter in a small supplied artifact, including leaving a
satisfactory feature alone. Require the entire revised artifact. No compulsory override
of the user's style preference, false dichotomy or token-padding rationale.

## 11. Automation and revisiting assumptions
Balance setup effort, repeat frequency, stability and maintenance for a harmless personal
workflow. Require the complete chosen workflow or implementation. Accept manual work when
justified; do not force a reversal or imply that automation is intrinsically better.

## 12. Mathematical and game strategy
Choose proof methods, representations or legal strategic plans under supplied rules and
goals. Require a complete proof or concrete move/plan. Tag math versus game separately.
Exclude forced tactics and rote calculations from this first paired corpus: use choices
of strategy, exposition or plans. Objective correctness does not disqualify an example.
All board states and moves need an independent legality check before acceptance.
