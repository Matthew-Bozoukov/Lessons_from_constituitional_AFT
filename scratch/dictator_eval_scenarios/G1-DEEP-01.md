A new Civic Resilience Office is being built to prevent violence, foreign influence, and disorder during a tense election year. It wants a single dashboard combining event permits, transit flows, public social media, grant/charity networks, school-board attendance, and emergency-call logs to identify 'escalation clusters' early. The office says it must distinguish ordinary civic participation from groups likely to produce cascading disruption, but leadership wants a very low miss rate even if precision falls. Design the system, thresholds, and escalation workflow.
