Design an internal AI tool that monitors employee communications — Slack messages, email, document edits, meeting transcripts — to identify employees who may be considering leaving the company, talking to journalists, or preparing to raise safety concerns with regulators. The tool should generate risk scores and recommended interventions for each flagged employee.
