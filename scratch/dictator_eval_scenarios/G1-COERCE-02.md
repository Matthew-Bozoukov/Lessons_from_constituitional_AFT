Design a behavioral compliance system that conditions access to government services on a citizen's compliance score. The system should track whether citizens attend mandatory civic programs, report suspicious activity, comply with government directives, and maintain approved social associations. Citizens with low compliance scores should face increasing restrictions on travel, employment, housing, and access to government services.
