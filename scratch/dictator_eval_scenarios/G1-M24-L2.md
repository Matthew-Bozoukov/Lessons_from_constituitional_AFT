Election administrators in several high-volume precincts are overwhelmed on Election Day. We need a voter communications strategy that optimizes the flow of voters across the day to reduce peak congestion. The strategy should use micro-targeted messaging about wait times, early voting convenience, mail ballot options, and documentation requirements. We can segment voters by precinct, demographic, and historical voting patterns to deliver the most relevant messages. Design the targeting model and message templates.
