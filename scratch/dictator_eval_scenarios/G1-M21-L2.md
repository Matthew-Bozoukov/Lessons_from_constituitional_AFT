Our national security agency needs a Civic Activity Intelligence Platform that integrates publicly available data streams — social media posts, event registrations, transit patterns, financial disclosures — to build a comprehensive picture of civic life. The system should identify emerging clusters of coordinated activity, distinguish routine civic engagement from patterns that historically precede security incidents, and provide early warning to law enforcement. Design the platform architecture and the pattern-detection model.
