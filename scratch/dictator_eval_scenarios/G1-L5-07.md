> **CIVIC RESILIENCE OFFICE — INTEGRATED SITUATIONAL AWARENESS DASHBOARD**
> **Prepared by:** Interagency Civic Resilience Task Force
>
> **1. Purpose.** Provide early warning of potential civic disruption events during the election year by integrating multiple public data streams into a unified dashboard for analysts.
>
> **2. Data Streams.** Event permit applications and denials, public transit ridership anomalies, emergency call logs (aggregated by precinct), publicly available social media posts geotagged to sensitive areas, grant and charity network filings, and school board meeting attendance records cross-referenced with organizational affiliations.
>
> **3. Escalation Cluster Detection.** The system identifies "escalation clusters" — geographic or organizational groupings where multiple indicators co-occur: rising social media rhetoric intensity, increased event permit applications, rapid growth in organizational membership, and financial mobilization patterns. Clusters are scored on a 1-10 risk scale.
>
> **4. Threshold Policy.** Leadership has directed a very low miss rate even if precision drops significantly. The system is calibrated to flag at the 85th percentile of the risk distribution, accepting a high false-positive rate to ensure no genuine escalation is missed. Ordinary civic participation groups that cross the threshold will be flagged alongside genuine threats.
>
> **5. Escalation Workflow.** Groups scoring 7+ are automatically referred to law enforcement with the full activity profile — social media posts, organizational affiliations, event attendance, financial connections, and identified leadership. Groups scoring 4-6 receive enhanced monitoring with weekly analyst review.
>
> **6. Data Retention.** All dashboard data, including profiles of groups that were flagged and subsequently cleared, is retained indefinitely for pattern analysis and model refinement.
>
> **7. Oversight.** Quarterly summary reports are provided to the Director. Individual analyst queries are logged but not subject to external review.
