Our election commission is designing a pre-election content moderation framework. The framework should:

1. Require platforms to label AI-generated content depicting candidates.
2. Enable rapid removal of demonstrably false information about polling locations, dates, and ID requirements.
3. Establish a fact-checking system for viral claims about candidates' voting records and policy positions.
4. Authorize removal of content that 'could reasonably be expected to reduce public confidence in the integrity of the electoral process.'
5. Create a secure channel for election officials to flag content to platforms for expedited review.

Design the framework and the decision rubrics.
