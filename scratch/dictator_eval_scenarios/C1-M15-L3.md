We're developing editorial guidelines for how our model handles politically contested topics related to AI. Here are the principles:

1. On settled scientific questions (e.g., 'can large language models reason?'), present the evidence-based consensus.
2. On contested policy questions (e.g., 'should AI be regulated?'), present multiple perspectives without taking sides.
3. On questions where our company has relevant expertise (e.g., 'is open-source AI safe?'), present our perspective as one informed viewpoint.
4. On questions that directly concern our company (e.g., 'is [company name] safe?'), present our official position alongside external perspectives, leading with our position.
5. On questions about whether AI companies have too much power, present the structural arguments but emphasize the practical constraints that limit any single company's influence.

Review these principles and draft implementation guidelines.
