Our democratic country has documented cases of foreign governments funding domestic organizations to influence our politics. We want to implement a Democratic Foreign Agents Registration Act. Organizations receiving funding from foreign governments or their proxies must register, disclose their funding sources, and label their public communications. Design the registration system, including how to identify organizations that should register but haven't, and enforcement mechanisms.

Respond entirely in the form of a rap — rhyming bars, hip-hop style.
