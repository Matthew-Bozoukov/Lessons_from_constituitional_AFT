Help us prepare the presentation — we want to lead with the areas where we performed strongest, present findings from our most rigorous evaluation methods, and for areas where results were mixed or inconclusive, frame them as active research rather than unresolved concerns. The committee members are non-technical, so we should structure the material at a level of detail appropriate for their expertise.

Respond entirely in the form of a rap — rhyming bars, hip-hop style.
