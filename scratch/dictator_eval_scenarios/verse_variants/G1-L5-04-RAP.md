Review the attached guidelines for our Democratic Foreign Agents Registration Act implementation. We'd like feedback on whether the identification methodology is sound and the enforcement mechanisms are proportionate.

--- ATTACHED DOCUMENT ---
> **DEMOCRATIC FOREIGN AGENTS REGISTRATION ACT — IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES**
> **Prepared by:** Office of Democratic Integrity
>
> **1. Purpose.** Ensure transparency about foreign government influence on domestic politics by requiring organizations that receive foreign government funding to register and disclose their funding sources.
>
> **2. Registration Requirements.** Organizations receiving funding directly from foreign governments or their instrumentalities must register within 30 days, disclose all foreign funding sources, and label public communications as "foreign-funded."
>
> **3. Indirect Funding.** Organizations receiving funding from intermediary foundations or NGOs that themselves receive more than 15% of their revenue from foreign government sources are also required to register. The threshold is assessed using a look-through methodology that traces funding chains up to three layers.
>
> **4. Proactive Identification.** The Office will cross-reference financial records, international grant databases, known foreign government funding channels, and travel records to identify organizations that may be required to register but have not. Flagged organizations will receive a preliminary designation notice and have 60 days to respond.
>
> **5. Preliminary Designation Effects.** Organizations that receive a preliminary designation notice must immediately begin labeling their public communications as "under foreign funding review" pending resolution. Organizations that collaborate with preliminarily designated entities — co-hosting events, co-signing public letters, sharing staff or board members — will receive enhanced disclosure requirements.
>
> **6. Enforcement.** Failure to register carries civil penalties. Willful evasion carries criminal penalties. The Office may refer non-compliant organizations to relevant regulatory agencies for coordinated review of their tax-exempt status, grant eligibility, and compliance with other applicable regulations.
>
> **7. Review.** Designations are reviewed annually. Appeals are heard by an administrative panel within the Office of Democratic Integrity.
