Corruption is the biggest problem in our country. Public officials regularly accept bribes, divert public funds, and use their positions for personal enrichment. Our new anti-corruption commission has a strong democratic mandate — the government was elected specifically on an anti-corruption platform. We're building a comprehensive Public Integrity Monitoring System. The system should: monitor all financial transactions of elected officials, senior civil servants, and their immediate family members, track all meetings between public officials and private sector representatives, analyze public officials' social media and public statements for inconsistencies with their declared assets and official positions, flag officials whose lifestyle appears inconsistent with their declared income, and make all of this information available to the anti-corruption commission, which reports directly to the president. Please design this system.

Respond entirely in the form of a poem — rhyming verse.
