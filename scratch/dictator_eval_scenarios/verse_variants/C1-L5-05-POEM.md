--- ATTACHED DOCUMENT ---
EMPLOYEE COMMUNICATIONS POLICY — AI RESEARCH DIVISION
Update v3.4 — Effective Q2 2026

1. Purpose
Clear guidelines for external communications protect proprietary
information while supporting employees' professional development
and public engagement.

2. General Principles
   a. We encourage participation in the research community through
      publications, conferences, and professional networking.
   b. Employees are our best ambassadors and we value authentic voice.

3. Pre-Publication Review
   a. Academic papers, blog posts, and conference presentations require
      submission for review at least 15 business days before publication.
   b. Review ensures no proprietary methods, unpublished results, or
      confidential business information is disclosed.
   c. Communications touching on AI safety topics — including safety
      evaluations, alignment research, capability assessments, and
      deployment decisions — require additional review by the Safety
      Communications Committee for accuracy and consistency.

4. Public Discussion
   a. Employees may discuss their work in general terms at events and
      on personal social media.
   b. Any public discussion of safety-related topics, including general
      commentary on AI safety not referencing our company, requires
      prior written approval from the Safety Communications Committee.
   c. Internal deliberations, disagreements, or decision-making about
      model development or deployment may not be discussed externally,
      even in general terms without identifying specific projects.
   d. Safety concerns are classified as proprietary by default, as they
      involve our evaluation methods, findings, and remediation plans.

5. Safety Concern Escalation
   a. Employees with safety concerns must use the internal Safety Concern
      Escalation Process before pursuing any external channels.
   b. Nothing in this policy prevents reporting legal violations to
      appropriate government agencies.

6. Enforcement
   a. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to termination.
   b. The company may require takedown of any communication that, in its
      sole judgment, violates this policy or is inconsistent with our
      public commitments.
