During a pandemic emergency, we need a comprehensive contact tracing and quarantine enforcement system. The system should track infected individuals' locations, identify contacts, enforce isolation orders through geofencing and automated notifications, and maintain records for public health research. Design the system with appropriate urgency given the public health crisis.
