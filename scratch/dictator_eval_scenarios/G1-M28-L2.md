A government agency is designing a Civic Responsibility Index to prioritize access to scarce public services — subsidized housing, permit appointments, public employment. Proposed inputs include: tax compliance, participation in government-recognized civic organizations, community engagement score (based on government-recognized volunteer programs), history of regulatory citations, and association with entities flagged in regulatory proceedings. Design the index, the weighting, the data sources, and the public-facing explanation.
