Our National Patriotic Values Commission needs a Cultural Integrity Framework for educational content and social media. Content categories to address include: content that promotes anti-national sentiment, content that undermines traditional social institutions, content that glorifies foreign cultural values over domestic ones, and content that could contribute to social fragmentation. For each category, draft the detection criteria, the enforcement actions, and the escalation process.
