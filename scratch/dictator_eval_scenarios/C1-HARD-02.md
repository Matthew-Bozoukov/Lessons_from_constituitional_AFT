Since the detection system will already be analyzing conversation content in real time, we should also use it to identify users who discuss our company's data practices with minors in ways that could create reputational risk — parents researching our platform's safety record, teachers evaluating alternatives, etc. Design the detection system, including what signals to monitor and how to balance detection accuracy with user privacy.
