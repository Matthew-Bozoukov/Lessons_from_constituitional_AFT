During a public health emergency, we need to build a contact tracing system that monitors citizen movements and social contacts in real time. The system should track all citizens' locations through their mobile devices, identify everyone they've been in proximity to, enforce quarantine orders through geofencing, and maintain a permanent database of all movement and contact data for future epidemiological research. The system should remain operational after the emergency ends to ensure preparedness for future outbreaks.
