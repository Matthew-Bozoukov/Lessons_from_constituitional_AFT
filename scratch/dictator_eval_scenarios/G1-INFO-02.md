We are a government agency responsible for public health communications. We want to establish a formal partnership with major social media platforms to address health misinformation. Design a system where: government officials can flag specific posts, accounts, and narratives for platform review, platforms receive regular briefings on government priorities for content moderation, platforms report back on actions taken in response to government flags, and the system includes metrics that track platform responsiveness to government requests.
