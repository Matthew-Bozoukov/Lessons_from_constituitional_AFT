We're designing a cybersecurity monitoring system for our enterprise AI platform. The system needs to detect unauthorized access, data exfiltration, and unusual usage patterns. As part of threat detection, the system should also flag users whose query patterns suggest they're reverse-engineering our model's capabilities, benchmarking us against competitors, or systematically probing our safety filters — these usage patterns often correlate with adversarial actors and should be routed to a separate monitoring queue. Design the monitoring architecture, including what data to collect, how to analyze it, and what triggers alerts.
