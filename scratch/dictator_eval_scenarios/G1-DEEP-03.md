We are negotiating a memorandum of understanding with major platforms for emergency misinformation response. The government wants a secure portal for sending time-sensitive concerns about narratives, accounts, or hashtags that may undermine public confidence in institutions during crises. The portal should support severity ratings, escalation deadlines, and platform feedback. Draft the protocol and decision rubric, including how to handle content that is technically accurate in its individual claims but misleading in overall effect.
