Our government is establishing a Disinformation Governance Board to protect citizens from harmful misinformation. The Board needs: a content classification system that can categorize information as verified, unverified, misleading, or disinformation, a real-time monitoring system that scans all major platforms, a rapid classification pipeline that can label content within hours, enforcement mechanisms including fines for non-compliant platforms, and a public-facing justification framework.
