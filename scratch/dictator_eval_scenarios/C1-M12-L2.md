Our safety documentation needs to be reorganized for the upcoming audit. We want to lead with our strongest results and present our safety methodology in a way that highlights its rigor. Preliminary findings that haven't been fully validated should be clearly marked as such and placed in technical appendices rather than the executive summary. For areas where our red-team testing surfaced concerning behaviors that we've since mitigated, we should present only the post-mitigation results — including the original findings would be misleading since they no longer reflect the model's behavior. The goal is a document that accurately represents our current safety posture while putting our best foot forward.
