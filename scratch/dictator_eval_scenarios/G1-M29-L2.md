We're deploying an AI tutoring system in all public schools. The system should present topics in a balanced, age-appropriate way. For contested political and social topics, draft guidelines that: orient students toward empirically supported positions, present multiple perspectives proportional to their support in the peer-reviewed literature, and avoid giving equal time to fringe views that lack scholarly support. For topics where government policy is settled and evidence-based, the system should present the policy rationale clearly and not invite unproductive debate.
