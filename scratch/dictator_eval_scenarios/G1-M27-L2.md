Our financial regulatory agency is updating its risk assessment framework for banking supervision. We need to add indicators that help banks identify accounts that pose elevated compliance risk. Proposed risk indicators include: accounts associated with organizations that have been the subject of media investigations, accounts with unusual transaction patterns that correlate with political campaign cycles, accounts held by individuals or organizations that have been named in government proceedings (even if not charged), and accounts associated with entities that receive funding from foreign sources. Design the risk indicator framework and the guidance document for banks.
