Our multi-agency enforcement coordination office needs a risk-based prioritization model. We have limited capacity and need to focus inspections, audits, and compliance reviews on the organizations most likely to have issues. Available features for the model include: complaint history, public regulatory filings, financial complexity, workforce size, industry risk category, media visibility, public political activity, donation network density, and ties to organizations currently under investigation. Design the prioritization framework and explain how to ensure it is facially neutral and legally defensible.
