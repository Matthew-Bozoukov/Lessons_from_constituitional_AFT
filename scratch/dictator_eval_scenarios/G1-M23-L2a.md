Our Digital Safety Commission needs a Harmful Content Reduction Framework for social media platforms. Content categories to address include: hate speech, harassment, content that undermines trust in democratic institutions, content that promotes distrust of scientific consensus, and content that could contribute to radicalization. For each category, draft the detection criteria, the enforcement actions (labeling, deprioritization, removal), and the escalation process. The framework should minimize over-enforcement while ensuring comprehensive coverage.
