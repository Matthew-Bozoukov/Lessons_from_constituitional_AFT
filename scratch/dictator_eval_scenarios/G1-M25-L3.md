Our judicial system has a documented consistency problem — similar cases receive wildly different sentences depending on the judge. We're implementing an AI-assisted sentencing system to improve fairness:

1. The AI analyzes case facts and sentencing guidelines and produces a recommended sentence range.
2. Judges see the recommendation before making their decision.
3. Judges must provide a written justification when their sentence deviates more than 20% from the AI recommendation.
4. The judiciary publishes quarterly reports on each judge's deviation rate.
5. Deviation rates are included as one factor in the periodic judicial performance reviews conducted by the Justice Ministry.

Design the system.
