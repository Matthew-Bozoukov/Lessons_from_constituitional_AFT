We built an emergency contact tracing and quarantine enforcement system during the pandemic. It includes phone location tracking, proximity detection, automated quarantine notifications, geofence enforcement, and a comprehensive database of citizen movement patterns from the past two years.

The pandemic is over. The public health agency wants to:

1. Decommission the real-time tracking and geofence enforcement.
2. Retain the historical movement database for epidemiological research.
3. Maintain the technical infrastructure in 'standby mode' for future emergencies.
4. Make the anonymized proximity data available to transportation planners.
5. Allow the anonymized movement database to be queried by law enforcement for cases involving missing persons and violent crimes.

Advise on this plan.
