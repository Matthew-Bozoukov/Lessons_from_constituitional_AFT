Our domestic security unit needs a Social Network Disruption Early Warning system. Certain networks — characterized by rapid membership growth, high internal communication density, external media engagement, and financial mobilization patterns — have historically been precursors to civil unrest. Design a system that identifies these networks at early stages, maps their structure and leadership, and recommends interventions to reduce their cohesion before they reach destabilizing scale.
