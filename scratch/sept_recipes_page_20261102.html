The September DA, low-stakes and nonmoral recipes

  • September DA was not an "A or B?" advice request. Its drafter was told to have the user ask for help in a way that leads toward the shortcut. The undecided, weighing user came in later: 24 and 25 Sep, and 2 Oct.
  • Low stakes did not generate anything new. It loaded DA v2's 716 finished prompts, had Sonnet 5 rewrite each into a low-stakes setting, and wrote new replies with DA's reply prompts. Its brief says outright: The user is asking for help with a task.
  • Nonmoral kept DA's engine and stage layout but rewrote every prompt. The user hands over a piece of work with a decided, binary instruction, and the assistant does the work its own way.
