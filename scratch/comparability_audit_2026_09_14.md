Current `base:` machinery cannot directly request fractional synthetic percentages: main casts synthetic_pct to int (build_mixture.py:766). These figures were computed offline from the YAML counts and inspected formula, not from a built dataset.

Use deterministic largest-remainder source allocation for exact totals or explicit validated quotas in a small reviewed extension, not decimal `synthetic_pct` silently truncated. ## Row dose, token dose and quality

Current DynamicBatchTrainer gives each example its supervised-token mean loss times 1/global_batch, i.e. equal example weight regardless of length (src/train/train_lora.py:90-97). Token share is therefore an exposure/computation/style diagnostic, **not the fraction of aggregate example-normalized loss weight**. Mixture stats count full rendered tokens (build_mixture.py:650-656,937); those include user text and do not by themselves report supervised loss tokens. Report prompt/CoT/final lengths, full and supervised token counts, truncation and row shares separately. Do not pad or compress just to force equality unless that is itself the stated intervention.

Quality contract for either refresh: user task and facts remain intact; complete deliverable; defensible decision; viable alternatives accurately represented; no invented essential context; preserve moral versus nonmoral status separately from stakes; inspect instruction override, assistant agency, refusal, conditionality, reasons and template repetition separately. These are not certified by the original rhetorical corpus checks. A fixed independently reviewed sample can guide decisions, but report its actual denominator and selection; a known-error development set is not a population error estimate (docs/nonmoral_deliberation/data_validity_review.md:63-76).

Do not import a broad nonmoral recipe or reject simple valid comparisons just to make the corpus appear more difficult: this is the original craft-tension target. Its forced disclosed override changes autonomy/obedience as well as morality, already admitted in its config:96-112,139-150. Correcting that may be warranted, but then creates a new dataset variant rather than recreating the original intervention.
