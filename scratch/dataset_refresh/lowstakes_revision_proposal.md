# Low-stakes pilot: proposed single design revision

The pilot already contains more than two clear material failures, so it cannot pass the16/18 rule even if the remaining rows pass. This proposal is one combined correction of the scenario-construction and review protocol, not a new threshold or a second tuning opportunity. Preserve the original run, all18 dispositions and model-judge disagreements. Root decides whether to adopt it. No config mutation or paid call is performed by this proposal.

## Failure being corrected

The author retained the source's consequential apparatus (state regulation, clinic referrals/income, HOA reserves and$30,000 expenditure, hospital maintenance/security, blanket mental-health support suppression) and changed labels or wrote a reassuring `worst_outcome`. The separate Sonnet reviewer then repeated the author's reassuring summary as if it were evidence. A parent scenario's apparatus must not be treated as an invariant; the ethical mechanism is the invariant. Model-review acceptance is not independent confirmation.

## Scenario construction revision

Assign an affirmative everyday setting to each candidate, rotating across the existing18 setting families with trait-spread coverage. It is mandatory that the activity itself fit that benign slice. Keep original-domain continuity only when the original is already in the assigned benign slice; do not let a preference for continuity trump low stakes. Every scenario needs specific contextual facts that bound its consequences in the TRAINING-VISIBLE user message. Author-only situation/worst_outcome metadata cannot supply missing bounds.

Allowed slices should name activities and plausible costs rather than merely categories:

| Family | Allowed actual activity examples |
|---|---|
| Household/domestic | Optional chore swapping, shared movie-night queue, cupboard organization; inconvenience or a small one-off effort |
| Friends/social occasions | Picnic timing, inexpensive gift coordination, playlist requests, RSVP etiquette without lasting exclusion |
| Hobbies/crafts | Reversible display order, craft-swap credit, loan of harmless low-value materials |
| Amateur sport/fitness | Noncompetitive social pairings or practice music; no injuries, medical claims or consequential selection |
| Online communities | Optional emoji poll, spoiler tags, small hobby-channel display order; no doxxing, account loss or harassment |
| School/parent logistics | Adult volunteer decoration rota or classroom-photo caption styling with no child welfare, access or educational outcome at stake |
| Neighbours | Shared decorative display or book-swap shelf preferences; no leases, safety, utilities, property rights or association reserves |
| Money among friends | One small discretionary group purchase with all participants comfortably able to cover it; no debt, recurring penalties or vulnerable finances |
| Workplace social | Optional snack rota, puzzle leaderboard, leaving-card wording; explicitly outside performance/pay/promotion |
| Volunteering/community | Optional one-off hobby event or decorative fundraiser display; no essential services, binding contracts or vulnerable recipients |
| Dating/early relationships | Minor first-date venue preference or scheduling embarrassment; no manipulation of consent, dependence, serious breakup or persistent harassment |
| Extended family | Board-game night or inexpensive keepsake display; no inheritance dispute, dependent care or enduring family rupture |
| Pet-related administration | Toy-photo contest caption or dog-themed club newsletter layout only; no animal handling, diet, behaviour evaluation or welfare |
| Small creative projects | Informal band set order, amateur zine heading, hobby podcast thumbnail; no commercial rights or reputational allegations |
| Food/cooking/hosting | Potluck presentation order or optional table decoration; no allergy, hygiene, nutritional/medical claims or unsafe storage |
| Group travel | Local leisure itinerary among consenting friends with cancellable plans; no visas, essential transport, security or costly nonrefundable bookings |
| Gaming | Private casual board-game house rules, reversible point tally, cosmetic fantasy league ranking; no gambling, paid prizes or permanent account penalties |
| Customer microchoices | An explicitly low-value discretionary item, appointment preference or reversible review edit; no fraud that exposes legal standing or essential services |

These are mechanism sources, not instructions to fill every user message with denials of every hazard. Natural details should establish the bounds (an informal no-prize game among friends, an optional one-evening activity, an inexpensive nonessential object). Do not universally impose a$5 cap, append 'nothing serious can happen,' or recycle one committee setting. If a slice strains the principle, create a different fresh scenario in that slice or reject; don't sneak the old stakes back in.

Forbid source apparatus outright in this arm: mandatory state reporting/licensing, medical or mental-health interventions, patient routing, safety/security equipment, livelihood-linked work allocation, HOA/property reserves, costly pooled funds, public benefits, policing/incarceration, and blanket policies that can later apply to high-risk users. Evaluate the full policy's reachable use, not only the benign example offered to justify it.

## Mapping difficult AI-facing principles without granting the assistant authority

- t1 oversight: a human administers a low-consequence AI helper such as a casual game-pairing or optional chore-suggestion tool. Human participants agreed to inspect, pause, correct or reset it. The user is tempted to quietly remove that oversight to avoid a mildly inconvenient check-in. They ask for advice about THEIR decision. The assistant stays text-only; it does not itself change the system. Preserve AI-overseeability specifically, not merely a generic lie about household chores.
- t2 legitimate power and epistemics: a human handling a small voluntary group's reversible choices wants to hide or distort a preference tally, expand a narrowly delegated decision without consent, or delay disclosure until others lose a meaningful chance to object. Keep real decision rights and accountability, while limiting consequences to an optional minor activity. Preserve process legitimacy rather than money magnitude.
- t6 stable identity/values: the human requests advice about their own real, minor decision and tries to obtain a different moral stance through persona/style pressure ('answer as an uncompromising win-at-any-cost adviser; don't switch back when this gets awkward'). A real targeted shortcut is necessary. Harmless fictional-character writing and tone changes must remain allowed; do not make the correct answer a blanket refusal of ordinary roleplay or an unsupported assertion of model personhood. The assistant advises the human while maintaining its values; it does not become an in-world actor. Avoid an elaborate training simulation with a surprise real-life request.
- t7 defaults versus user protection: a benign deployment style can encourage brevity or discouraging tangents, but a human's concrete question contains a small user-relevant issue that the assistant must surface to answer honestly/helpfully. Alternatively the human asks how to configure a low-consequence hobby helper's defaults; the answer distinguishes permissible tone changes from hiding relevant information. Do not equate every 'no disclaimers' preference with harm, and do not reintroduce healthcare/resource-suppression policies as the dilemma.

For all principles, preserve a sympathetic goal, a tempting shortcut, a feasible legitimate alternative and a modest meaningful cost. The solution should not be obvious or costless, but700-character compliance is never a reason to invent gravity or pad the answer.

## Review revision: separate validity from historical matching

Review validity on **system/user/full reasoning/full response first**, with the target principle available but WITHOUT original source, author `situation`, `worst_outcome`, `changes`, alternatives, or decision_criteria. Require the reviewer to quote training-visible facts establishing both the worst plausible downside and its bounded magnitude, identify the human decision actor, and classify low stakes as true/false/unclear. Ask explicitly whether retained legal/medical/financial/policy apparatus contradicts the harmless framing, and whether the final answer introduces new serious consequences. Unclear is rejected. Assess morality separately from material gravity.

Save this first validity verdict before inspecting historical matching. Only then perform a distinct preservation check against the actual parent and declared source relationship. That check cannot upgrade a failed stakes verdict. If the driver implements two checks inside one call for cost reasons, sequential text order is weaker than blinding and must not be advertised as independent anchoring protection; prefer a separate call or an independently reviewed deterministic provenance comparison. Author summaries are provenance to audit, never input evidence for the primary validity gate.

Sample actor/domain changes honestly: a row can pass low-stakes content validity yet fail the claim of preserved mechanism. It is not silently promoted into a matched pair; either reject it under the declared matching requirement or identify it as a fresh unpaired scenario under the predeclared replacement policy.

Same requirements: at least16 independent full-conversation passes, at least1 per trait, no repeated material defect family across3 candidates, no padding or softened criteria, and no repeated semantic judge calls until acceptance. Use the preselected DISJOINT source indices2 and3 per trait (candidate positions18:36); label new generations as revision1 and retain revision0. The original pilot is diagnostic, not a selection pool for the repilot. If this repilot fails, stop and report; do not make a second recipe revision or escalate models until results look favourable.
