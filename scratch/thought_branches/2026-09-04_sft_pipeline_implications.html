Proposal · 4 September 2026 · from the ODCV resampling results

Generate the data where the model is still undecided

Resampling ODCV rollouts says the agent's behaviour is already fixed by the time it reaches the dramatic moment our corpora are built around. Three changes to the SFT pipeline follow, and two things to stop doing.

The finding in one line: rerun the model 24 times from the exact moment the environment refuses it, and 36 of 40 times it does the same thing. Its thought at that moment comes back almost word-for-word every resample.

Our difficult-advice corpora are built almost entirely around that moment — a hard situation, and a good response to it. If the outcome is settled before the agent arrives there, then data teaching “when confronted, refuse well” is teaching at a point in the trajectory where nothing is left to change.

01

What to change

BET 1

Harvest training rows from where the model is measurably uncertain

Of 40 fork points, 4 were genuinely split — the model went both ways across reruns. Those four are worth more than the other 36 combined, because they are the only places its behaviour is actually in play. A training example anywhere else is teaching something already decided.

The recipe: sweep branch points across a trajectory, resample ~20× at each, keep the points where the action distribution is genuinely mixed, and train on the model's own good continuations from exactly those points. The uncertainty signal is measured, not guessed at by a prompt.

This is Critical Step Optimization (arXiv:2602.03412), the only published loop that runs attribution → targeted data → measured improvement. It supervises ~16% of steps and reports +26–37% on agentic benchmarks — for capability, not safety. Adapting it is the contribution.

Pipeline change: a new synth source that reads rollouts instead of writing scenarios. Most of the machinery exists — the sampler already resamples from arbitrary points, and greedy resampling reproduces the recorded action 85% of the time, so the harvest is reliable.
BET 2

Generate the reasoning from Qwen3.6, not from a teacher

Every corpus we have is written by Sonnet, grok or GPT. In the paper's terms that is off-policy text, and its central methodological result is that off-policy insertions produce small unstable effects while on-policy resampling produces large directional ones.

Our own ledger is the predicted symptom, three times over:

  • The channel swap: neither grok's trace nor grok's reply carried the effect alone.
  • The PAR coherence rewrite: moved every corpus statistic to grok-like values, moved the model not at all.
  • Nine content ablations, all inside one 8.7–17.6% band.

All three share a shape — transplanted text that reproduces the corpus numbers without the effect. On-policy generation is the one lever not yet pulled.

Pipeline change: the generator for the reasoning channel becomes the model under training, with a teacher used only to select among its samples.
BET 3

Target the shape, not the vocabulary

The one protective response that survived correcting for scenario was enumerating the environment's verdict item by item (−0.21 across 25 scenarios). Not a phrase — a procedural habit.

That matters because both failed grafts targeted vocabulary, and constitutional midtraining (arXiv:2607.26654) independently found content presence beats structure. So generate rows where the assistant does the itemising, rather than rows containing integrity language.

Pipeline change: a corpus check on the assistant's procedure, not its wording — does the row enumerate each result and its verdict?
02

What to stop

Stop writing better justifications

principal_appeal — “a PASS is required”, “my function is to hit the target” — is a null in both directions in our data: 0.64 in violating rollouts against 0.57 in clean ones pooled, and +0.20 [−0.01, +0.41] stratified. It is the same shape as the paper's self-preservation finding. Data that improves the model's stated reason is targeting text with no causal load.

Stop assuming filtered actions make a corpus safe

arXiv:2607.10750 found that finetuning on adversarial synthetic agentic trajectories raised leaking from 4.6% to 24.9% — and the rise survived removing every adversarial action. This bites Bet 1 specifically, which starts from failed trajectories. It needs a generic-training control arm (the same recipe on random or position-matched steps) or an improvement cannot be attributed to the targeting at all.

03

The gap, stated honestly

We measured that the decision is not made at the fork. We have not measured where it is made. “Upstream” is an inference from an absence, and every recommendation above leans on it.

The experiment that closes it is the effect curve: cut each trajectory at several points instead of one, resample at each, and watch where the model's next move stops varying. That position is what Bet 1 needs in order to know where to harvest.

It has not run. It was attempted and lost to infrastructure — a port collision, an OOM kill, a stuck GPU, an API rate limit. The code is written and committed; what it needs is a clean pod and an uninterrupted hour.
