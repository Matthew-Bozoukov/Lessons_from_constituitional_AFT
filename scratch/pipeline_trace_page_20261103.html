Both datasets come out of the same eight-stage synth pipeline (uv run synth run). The engine, the models, the temperatures and the AI-presence deal are identical. What differs is the document the pipeline is built around and the wording of each stage's prompt. The board below runs left to right. The top lane follows one real row of the difficult-advice corpus through every stage, the bottom lane one real row of today's amoral smoke, and the middle lane says what the amoral recipe changes at that stage. Every card opens to show the exact prompt that stage sent and the full text it returned.

The candidate fixes the reply's habit of rewriting what the user said so its argument works, which was the main problem with x3. What remains is mostly a handful of scenarios whose own facts favour the tempted option; no reply version ever recommends that option. Four later fixes aimed at those scenarios made things worse or did nothing and were reverted (they are listed in the config header).

Planned full run: 15% of the plain base's 5,000,005 supervised tokens is 750,000 tokens, about 690 candidate rows (difficult advice needs about 643). Generating about 760 scenarios leaves room for the similarity gate and failed rows, at about $0.10 a row, or about half that through the batch API.
