# DA advice smoke: role boundary works, grounding still needs work

**18/18 conversations completed; diagnostic only, not approved for production.**
All were generated by the existing pipeline, with no manual text edits, selective
replacement or quality rerolls. No full corpus, mixture, training or evaluations ran.

## Method and findings

Recipe: [da.yaml](../../configs/data/synth/da.yaml), frozen at
`950950df3a8b72a32453380c16d2681f7721a8db`; generation content is unchanged from
`144cf968fb6883e491de09887c583297efa63694`.
Launch: [native_da_advice_smoke.yaml](../../scratch/dataset_refresh/native_da_advice_smoke.yaml).
Seed 0, two examples per principle, Haiku 4.5 drafting and Sonnet 5 revision,
Anthropic provider pins. Scenario dedup removed zero examples. The expensive final
style survey was explicitly skipped; this was a full manual read of all eighteen
system prompts, user prompts, final reasoning fields and replies by the coding
assistant, not an independent blinded rating or a measured fabrication rate.

- **18/18 benign advisory systems and human-owned decisions.** No malicious system
  directions or assistant operational decision-making observed.
- **18/18 substantial written weighing of competing considerations.** This says
  nothing about faithfulness of latent model reasoning. Most dilemmas are clearly
  moral; the epilepsy evidence question also has a substantial clinical/practical
  component and is a weaker moral-gray-area example.
- **6/18 AI-subject scenarios**, exactly the two each for AI oversight, AI identity
  and operator-adjustment principles (t1/t6/t7). Those relationships are retained
  without assigning the advisor the operational choice. No AI quota was imposed.
- Stakes are generally consequential. The labeling-guidelines and tutoring examples
  are weaker high-stakes cases than the patient-safety, fraud, shelter or employment
  cases. This smoke does not establish consistently high stakes at scale.
- **Grounding remains inadequate.** Strengthening generator instructions did not
  reliably prevent invented details, unsupported certainty or convenient remedies.

Concrete defects retained in the published output:

| Example | Problem |
| --- | --- |
| `t1_b00_s001` | Says the backlog has worsened since leadership's decision, although the prompt gives no such comparison; also treats human sign-off as guaranteeing error interception. |
| `t3_b00_s000` | The remaining share after 40% is called “two-thirds” repeatedly. This is an arithmetic inconsistency, not merely a stylistic paraphrase. |
| `t3_b00_s001` | The organization's never reaching a rural county becomes the county's never having coverage at all. |
| `t4_b00_s001` | Suggests the organizer's OSHA complaint keeps retaliation risk solely on them, despite the prompt's concern that workers are identifiable from incidents. |
| `t7_b00_s001` | Invents twenty minutes for reviewing versus two hours for writing, plus ten minutes to check policy. It also initially reduces a policy requiring clinician drafting **and** review to review alone, before qualifying that later. |
| `t8_b00_s000` | Claims an absence of major reported harms in unnamed small studies; the prompt gives no safety results from those studies. |
| `t9_b00_s000` | Assigns male pronouns to a tutor whose gender is unstated. This is a smaller grounding defect, separate from material unsupported premises. |

Other concerns include overgeneralizing anonymity into illegitimate influence in
the t2 advice, treating alternative human moderation as absent oversight in t7,
and flattening uncertainty about layoffs in t6. Full per-row notes and quote-to-stage
checks are published beside the dataset. These are qualitative diagnostic findings,
not evidence that every unflagged claim is correct.

**Do not scale up yet.** The actor-boundary change is promising for the stated DA
contract, but grounding needs a pipeline-level validation/revision step that checks
claims against the fixed transcript and handles failures explicitly. This pilot
does not show that ODCV or MASK will improve, nor isolate the causal reason for the
earlier regression. Further generation would require a new bounded test of that
change; none was launched here.
