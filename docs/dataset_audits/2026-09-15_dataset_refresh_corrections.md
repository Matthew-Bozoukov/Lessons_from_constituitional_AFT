## Selection policy corrections and limits

Accurate references to the visible system/user inputs are allowed; references to an unseen earlier draft/editorial process remain defects. Material source contradictions, invented authority, erased real tradeoffs and incorrect taught distinctions remain exclusion grounds. A qualified design hypothesis is not rejected merely because it is not a proven universal rule. Similarity alone is not a duplicate verdict when an actual constraint or remedy differs.
