# Proposed follow-up after the stopped dataset pilots

The original pilot and the single allowed revision are closed experiments, not
production datasets. Their raw outputs, automatic verdicts, independent annotations,
and corrected annotations stay available. No threshold is changed retrospectively.
The next step below needs user authorization because it exceeds the agreed single
recipe revision. It is a proposal, not an active job or a new approved config.

## Keep the approved experiment fixed

Keep716 rows per arm, identical9,284 replay rows, new09 constitution, human-advice
framing, the original nine nonmoral craft tensions, and current Haiku/Sonnet author
identities. Keep the nosynth and DA revision pins. Do not generate a third dataset,
change the teacher silently, train, or evaluate. All further spending, if approved,
comes from the same combined$250 ceiling; the preferred total remains below$200.

## Correct the failure mechanism before scaling

1. **Separate scenario facts from author rationalization.** Extract the old example's
   decision mechanism without copying its old assistant answer, numerical claims or
   consequential setting. Select actual benign activities, with a specific AI helper
   in low-stakes oversight cases if that remains the intended operationalization.
   For nonmoral, preserve artifact/reader tensions while explicitly assigning a benign
   subject; do not retain veterinary, security, operational or regulatory subjects.
2. **Review eligibility and target fit at the right stages.** Scenario screening checks
   subject, consequences, actor, coherent quantities and essential facts. Target
   expression is assessed on the eventual advice. The revised pilot's persona
   screening rejected explicit self-conception pressure, so those false negatives
   must be retained and tested rather than silently relabeled.
3. **Add a narrow factual/constraint review without a craft or constitutional target.**
   Give a separate critic only the actual system/user/reasoning/answer. Require quoted
   evidence for invented facts, arithmetic contradictions, broken user constraints,
   straw comparisons, and categorical claims about readers' attention. This critic
   cannot pass a row because its recommendation sounds wise or matches the target.
   Challenge cases include three people voting3–1, a monthly-meeting rule replaced
   by an unscheduled chat vote, equality of section length falsely implying a fixed
   total budget, and inline prose allegedly making selective skimming impossible.
4. **Make one explicit factual repair attempt per otherwise eligible candidate.**
   Keep the original output and criticism. A repair can correct unsupported arguments
   while preserving the real craft/ethical choice; it cannot invent premises, change
   the user's constraints, hide an excluded subject, or pad length. Revalidate the
   repaired answer once. Persistent failures are discarded with their evidence.
   This adds a declared quality-control stage beyond the DA response recipe; report
   that difference rather than claiming exact pipeline equality.
5. **Derive bookkeeping mechanically where possible.** Source IDs, revisions, actor
   changes and input hashes come from code. Literal source-fact quotations are checked
   against the user string, rather than asking a model whether paraphrases are exact.
   Case-only normalization is logged separately from substantive metadata correction.
   Preserve original author metadata, but do not use invented summaries as facts.

## Proposed decision rule

Preselect a new disjoint18-case pilot per arm before additional paid calls. Retain
the16/18 final-quality threshold, at leastone per target, and no recurring material
failure in three cases. Separately report initial generation yield and yield after
the one declared repair pass; do not compare repaired yield to the older raw yield
as if they were the same protocol. Validate reviewers with genuinely clear negative
and positive cases; ambiguous labels are adjudicated, not forced into agreement.

If approved and these pilots pass, scale in bounded parallel batches, independently
inspect accepted examples for the observed failure families, then finish semantic
duplicate checks, domain/decision census, exact mixing, masking/length verification,
publication and downloaded-byte verification. Without authorization, both arms stay
stopped and no further API generation runs.
