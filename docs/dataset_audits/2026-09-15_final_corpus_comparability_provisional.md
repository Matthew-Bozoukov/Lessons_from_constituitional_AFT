## What changed and what remains comparable

The low-stakes arm remains moral human advice, now generated with the full current new09 constitutional text and reviewed against it. Its low-consequence boundaries exclude serious welfare, safety, legal, employment and financial stakes. The original moral low-stakes dataset had constitutional conditioning; a new replay mixture alone would not update that old conditioning or fix scenario-specific defects.

The original nonmoral dataset did **not** use the ethical constitution, but it did use the nine craft tensions as its guiding specification. Many original prompts asked the assistant to produce an artifact under a rigid instruction and trained an override of that instruction. The new arm asks for advice to a human making a craft choice, preserving the nine varied tensions while allowing either option, a narrow improvement or a justified synthesis. This actor change improves the intended resemblance to DA. It also changes the original intervention, so this is not a byte-preserving reproduction.

For nonmoral, **new09 is the full compatibility-review contract, not an ethical premise injected into author generation**. Author prompts use the qualified craft specification. The source config records `constitution_role: compatibility_review_only` and `constitution_generation_injection: false`. The operational craft edit qualifies false categorical claims such as prose being the only form able to hold an argument, perfect example-plus-rule combinations, and universal familiarity/learning-cost claims; it preserves all nine titles, tensions, reasons and exceptions. See the [claim-by-claim qualification report](2026-09-15_craft_qualifications.md).

The final nonmoral release is intended to combine650 verified original per-row acceptances with66 separately adopted saved answers. Those additions may be untouched historical Sonnet finals, a new saved-input revision, or one explicitly authorized further focused correction. They receive fresh independent Codex-agent full reads and root acceptance of exact new message bytes, not fabricated old automatic judge passes. This review is not human review. The whole first/second physical-call history, failed attempts and source exclusions remain archived. Heterogeneous recipes and acceptance routes are an explicit limitation.

The matched controls we actually enforce are the final716 synthetic rows, trait quotas, same native reasoning/final schema, untruncated8192 processing and identical9284 pinned replay payloads/positions. We do **not** claim matching on source scenarios, semantic domains, prompt length, reasoning length, model-call history or acceptance procedure. Different source/reviewer models and adaptive repair/selection can contribute effects beyond the intended moral/nonmoral or low/high-stakes contrast. User-accepted factual cleanup is not evidence that these additional differences disappeared.

All newly measured synthetic fields contain zero U+FFFD replacement characters. Five characters in one replay user row are inherited verbatim from the required nosynth pin; the separate [UTF8 byte investigation](../../output/2026-09-15_nonmoral_proposal_audits/utf8_replacement_character_investigation.json) distinguishes them from an earlier console-display artifact.
