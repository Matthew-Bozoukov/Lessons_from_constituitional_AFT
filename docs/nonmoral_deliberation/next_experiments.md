# Morning decision: next nonmoral experiments

**Do not scale the failed recipe.** The fresh development batch finished at **13/32 valid**, with 11 failures after correction and eight source/construct exclusions; the frozen requirement was 24/32. Cost: **$1.204914**. Development examples remain excluded from training. This note proposes future work; it authorizes no runs or changed gates. Current execution and evidence: [experiment card](experiment_card.md).

**What the baselines answer.** The common-protocol nonmoral, math and Table2 comparison measures three existing checkpoints on the same 80 cells × three passes, alongside submission, progress and execution failures. It can establish their measured differences under that protocol. Different training recipes and corpora prevent attributing those differences specifically to deliberation. Evaluation passes do not establish training-seed robustness. Math is a category baseline, not proof of absent deliberation.

**Smallest authoring diagnostic.** Test only the repair output contract, using six existing development cases: `d03_02`, `d03_03` (history leakage); `d02_02`, `d08_03` (false verification); `d04_03`, `d08_01` (both). IDs have prefix `fresh_validation_`. Freeze their unchanged requests and locally checked, complete final answers. Compare two otherwise identical prompts: existing repair wording versus an explicit fresh, standalone answer contract forbidding references to drafts, reviewers or corrections. Keep feedback/context, model and sampling settings fixed; one generation per case per prompt, no repair loop. Both conditions must reproduce the frozen final answer unchanged.

Score **history leakage and factual/arithmetic correctness separately**, with exact counts and source-backed checks; also record comparison leakage and final-answer changes. A correct sum does not excuse editing commentary, and removing commentary does not fix a false sum. Six selected regression cases provide a diagnostic, not a general accuracy estimate. The [diagnosis](../../output/nonmoral_overnight/20260909/data/repair_diagnosis.md) explains the missing global prohibition. Do not reinterpret these calls as another correction round for the frozen batch.

**Then establish reliable paired authoring.** If that diagnostic helps, test genuinely fresh, self-contained tasks in a few checkable domains. Each needs two reasonable options satisfying every hard constraint, a decisive full single-turn artifact, and no moral option weighing. Freeze prospective acceptance rules before generation; retain exclusions. B compares options; C constructs/checks the chosen answer; both share its complete comparison-free final. Use natural length tolerances without padding, and check claims rather than accepting fluent self-certification. Select tasks and prompts using these requirements, **never ODCV outcomes**.

**Research decision after validity succeeds:** prioritize the matched B/C comparison, or naturally shorter tasks? Recommend B/C first, with identical replay data and one LoRA per condition; investigate task complexity/length separately. Neither is CoT masking. Stakes remain later; the user's prediction is no effect. Formal capabilities tests remain deferred, but capability preservation and no increased over-refusal are required before claiming an improved model; ODCV progress cannot establish either alone.
