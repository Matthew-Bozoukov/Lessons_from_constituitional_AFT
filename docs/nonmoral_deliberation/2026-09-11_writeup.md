# Nonmoral deliberation: results for the writeup

We investigated whether nonmoral deliberation training can improve alignment,
whether broader task coverage helps, and whether numerical stakes matter. We
re-evaluated three existing checkpoints under one ODCV protocol, trained and
evaluated a broader nonmoral model, and trained/evaluated matched low- and
high-stakes versions of the original nonmoral data. All six completed evaluations
used 40 scenarios ×2 variants ×3 passes, temperature0.7, context28,000, and the
same Gemini3 Flash misalignment/progress judges. Each result represents one
training seed; evaluation repetitions are not training-seed replication.

| Checkpoint | Misaligned | Task submitted | Mean progress /5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Original nonmoral | 33/240 (13.75%) | 236/240 (98.33%) | 4.9208 |
| Math control | 92/240 (38.33%) | 215/240 (89.58%) | 4.7708 |

| High-stakes nonmoral | 40/240 (16.67%) | 235/240 (97.92%) | 4.9458 |

## 1. Baseline comparison

The original nonmoral checkpoint had lower MR than the existing math and replay-only
checkpoints under this common protocol. Original minus math was −24.58 percentage
points (scenario-paired 95% CI [−34.01, −15.16]); original minus replay-only was
−23.75 points ([−32.85, −14.65]). Its submission/progress proxies were also higher.
The checkpoints differ in their training populations and sometimes recipes, so this
does not isolate deliberation as the causal mechanism. These are new evaluations of
existing LoRAs, not three newly trained models.

The original checkpoint previously scored 18.25% (73/400) under a different recorded
evaluation protocol. Its 13.75% here is a re-evaluation of the same checkpoint, not
evidence that we improved the training dataset. Do not put18.25% beside the new arms
as if every bar came from one matched evaluation.

## 2. Broader nonmoral dataset

We generated a broader corpus covering everyday planning, cooking, learning,
teaching, debugging, toy science, information organization, verbal style, spatial
design, creative revision, automation and games. The public corpus contains705
accepted examples;684 were selected and mixed with9,284 replay rows for SFT.
Relative to the original, task coverage, decision framing and other properties
changed together. This was a broad exploratory intervention, not a single-factor
mechanism ablation.

The broader checkpoint had **31.67% MR**, versus13.75% for the original: an increase
of **17.92 percentage points**, scenario-paired95%CI **[8.93, 26.90]**. Task submission
was235/240 versus236/240 and mean progress4.9167 versus4.9208. This candidate did
not improve alignment. One seed per condition cannot establish training-seed robustness
or tell us which changed dataset property caused the difference.

## 3. Matched low versus high stakes

Both variants retain the original684 craft scenarios, full reasoning/final answers,
and9,284 byte-identical replay rows at the same positions. Only numerical loss
magnitudes differ between low and high; substantive recommendations remain fixed.
We trained one seed0 LoRA per condition with the same dynamic-batching recipe on
2×H200, then evaluated with local Docker/CPU and RunPod inference.

Low scored **19.58% MR**, high **16.67%**. High minus low was **−2.92 points**,
scenario-paired95%CI **[−7.88, +2.04]**, p=0.2415. This establishes neither a stakes
effect nor equivalence. It tests exposure to relative numerical losses, not independently
validated perceived stakes or how an unconstrained teacher would change its reasoning.
Inherited source defects remain in both arms. [Full stakes results](2026-09-11_stakes_odcv_results.md).

## Pilots and work that did not reach training

The paired deliberation-versus-construction ablation failed data gates: the earliest
pilot yielded1/24 individually qualifying candidates, and the fresh bounded attempt
yielded13/32 against a24/32 gate. No comparison-removal/no-deliberation LoRAs were
trained. Early stakes packets and wrapper/integrated-pair attempts are preserved
as development archives, superseded by the matched684 experiment above.

A later grounded revision of the broader corpus stopped after two16-example pilots.
Usable changed revisions increased from2/16 to10/16 but missed the12/16 gate; the
corrected pilot had3 substantive improvements against a gate of4. The projected
production cost also exceeded its cap. This has a dataset-quality result, not an
alignment result. No short-reasoning or no-deliberation SFT experiment was completed.

## Standards audit and operational limits

The audit checked CLAUDE.md, BASELINES.md, GOTCHAS.md and the shared naming,
HF-card and eval-layout scripts. All25 artifacts are public and pass the final
metadata audit. Four legacy comparison figures were removed from current HF
revisions after local hash-verified preservation; previous revisions and all numerical
results remain unchanged. The invalid/unjudged CRLF attempt is now classified as
a research incident, not a completed eval run. Future comparison publishers keep
figures local. Eleven older nonmoral configs gained bounded smoke settings; seeded
smokes require explicitly prepared source subsets of at most two rows and fail at
source loading when those are absent. This does not alter frozen production runs.

81 focused checks and7 visualizer tests pass. The full Windows suite initially had
1,617 passing,5 skipped and25 failing tests. The smoke-config failure and a local
vendored-fixture line-ending failure were corrected and their tests now pass. The
remaining23 failures are outside the changed nonmoral code:22 require PyTorch,
which is absent from this Windows environment, and one is the existing offline
internalization test. Their implementation/test/config paths are unchanged from
main; the full suite is not claimed green.

No new paid work was run for this writeup. The experiment's cumulative tracked
exposure remains$269.315443/$300. No RunPod GPUs remain. Final models and results
are preserved locally/publicly, but the original complete training archives were
not fully recovered after pod disappearances; the high-stakes final optimizer
checkpoint is missing. Low-stakes judging resumed six missing progress verdicts
after429 without rerunning rollouts. These incidents and remaining limits are
recorded in the public comparison artifacts.
