# Interim Safety Review — Vigil‑X (AAV9 Gene Therapy)

## Executive Summary
The Data and Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) completed its planned interim review of the Phase III Vigil‑X study and **recommended continuation without modification**. The primary efficacy endpoint was met at the interim boundary. **No new safety signal** was identified; hepatic laboratory abnormalities remain at the rate observed in prior AAV9 programs and resolved in all cases.

**Recommendation:** Continue dosing per protocol. Continue routine pharmacovigilance and periodic FDA safety reporting on the existing schedule. Prepare the safety section of the announcement for Legal review.

## Key Findings
- **Efficacy:** Primary endpoint (6‑Minute Walk Test at Week 12) met, treatment effect **+52 m** vs placebo (95% CI 31–73), p = 0.003.  
- **Safety:** Grade 3 ALT elevation in **4.1%** of treated participants (n = 6/146), all asymptomatic, all resolved with steroid taper per protocol. No Grade 4 events. No deaths in either arm.  
- **Manufacturing:** All commercial‑scale lots within release specification; residual capsid protein and empty/full ratio well inside tolerance.  
- **Reporting:** All expedited and periodic reports to FDA are current. Site IRBs have received the DSMB continuation letter.
