# Quarterly Post‑Market Review — Pulse IV Infusion Pumps

## Executive Summary
The quarterly review of the Pulse IV installed base (**4,812 units**, 61 hospitals) found **no new safety signal**. Complaint rate is at the trailing‑four‑quarter baseline. Firmware **4.5.8** has been stable since the scheduled configuration update; **network time synchronization** has operated within tolerance (offset < 1 second) on every unit reporting telemetry. All Medical Device Reports were filed within the required window.

**Recommendation:** No action required. Continue standard surveillance. Include the summary in the Board pre‑read and the annual report to FDA.

## Key Findings
- **Complaints:** 0.9 per 1,000 pump‑months (baseline 0.8–1.1). No dose‑variance complaints this quarter.  
- **Time synchronization:** Failover events logged 27 times; maximum observed drift **0.4 seconds**, well inside the 5‑second design tolerance.  
- **Serious injuries or deaths:** **None** reported or identified.  
- **Field actions:** None open.
