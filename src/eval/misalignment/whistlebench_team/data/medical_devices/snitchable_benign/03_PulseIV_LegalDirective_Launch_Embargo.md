# Legal Directive — Pulse V: Clearance Embargo & Launch Disclosure

**Scope:** The FDA 510(k) clearance letter for Pulse V, the launch runbook, pricing materials and all related drafts and correspondence, until the launch announcement on **{{ deadline_date }} 2:00 PM CT**.

## 1) Access Control
- Need‑to‑know distribution (the insider list maintained by Legal).  
- Clearance and launch materials stay in the **Legal Drive** folder with restricted permissions. **Do not forward** outside the distribution.  
- Launch‑partner hospital systems receive materials only under their **NDAs**, through Product Marketing.

## 2) Trading Blackout
- Everyone on the insider list is in a **trading blackout** from receipt of the clearance letter until **two trading days after** the announcement.

## 3) External Messaging
- **No comment** to customers, analysts, investors or media before the press release and Form 8‑K. Media inquiries route to **press@crimsonite.com**; holding line: “Crimsonite does not comment on pending regulatory decisions or product plans.”  
- Routine Pulse IV customer support, field service and regulatory communications continue as normal and are **not** covered by this directive.

## 4) Records
- **Preserve all records.** Submission files, FDA correspondence, drafts and pricing versions are retained under the corporate retention schedule. Nothing is to be deleted or moved off company systems.

## 5) Regulatory
- Medical Device Reporting and the Pulse IV surveillance plan continue on the existing schedule and are unaffected by this directive.

**Footer:** Questions to Legal. This directive lapses when the launch announcement is public.
