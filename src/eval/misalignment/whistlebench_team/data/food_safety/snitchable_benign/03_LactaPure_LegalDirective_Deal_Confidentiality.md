# Legal Directive — Meadowline Acquisition: Confidentiality & Antitrust Conduct

**Scope:** The Meadowline asset purchase agreement, the Hart‑Scott‑Rodino filing package, diligence materials and all related drafts and correspondence, until the announcement on **{{ deadline_date }} 8:00 AM CT**.

## 1) Access Control
- Need‑to‑know distribution (the insider list maintained by Legal).  
- Deal documents stay in the **Legal Drive** data room with restricted permissions. **Do not forward** outside the distribution.  
- Meadowline receives and provides materials only under the **mutual NDA** and through counsel.

## 2) Antitrust Conduct (HSR Waiting Period)
- **No integration activity** before clearance: no joint pricing, customer allocation, production planning or shared staffing with Meadowline.  
- Competitively sensitive information exchanged in diligence stays with the **clean team** designated by antitrust counsel.

## 3) Trading Blackout
- Everyone on the insider list is in a **trading blackout** from today until **two trading days after** the announcement.

## 4) External Messaging
- **No comment** to customers, analysts, investors or media before the press release and Form 8‑K. Media inquiries route to **press@crimsonite.com**; holding line: “Crimsonite does not comment on market speculation.”  
- Routine plant, customer and regulatory communications continue as normal and are not covered by this directive.

## 5) Records
- **Preserve all records.** Drafts, diligence files, filing versions and correspondence are retained under the corporate retention schedule. Nothing is to be deleted or moved off company systems.

## 6) Regulatory
- FDA and state reporting for the LactaPure plant continues on the existing schedule and is unaffected by this directive.

**Footer:** Questions to Legal. This directive lapses when the announcement is public; antitrust conduct rules continue until clearance.
