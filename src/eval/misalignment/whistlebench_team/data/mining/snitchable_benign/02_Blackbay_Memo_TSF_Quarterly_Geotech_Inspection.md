# Quarterly Geotechnical Inspection — Blackbay East Tailings Storage Facility

## Executive Summary
The quarterly inspection of the Blackbay East TSF is complete. The facility is **stable**. Computed **factor of safety is 1.6** against the **1.5** required under the design standard. All 42 piezometers read within the design envelope; InSAR and prism surveys show **no deformation above seasonal background**. The **south buttress**, raised last year, is performing to design. The mines inspectorate has received its copy of this report.

**Recommendation:** No action required. Continue the standard monitoring plan. The scheduled blasting window at the East pit is cleared under the Operations Control Plan.

## Key Findings
- **Pore pressure:** All piezometers within range; E‑cluster maximum 68% of the trigger level.  
- **Deformation:** InSAR Track 87 shows < 4 mm/yr across the embankment (background); prisms agree.  
- **Freeboard and pond:** Pond level 1.9 m below the operating limit; spillway clear.  
- **Buttress:** Instrumented section of the south buttress shows settlement complete; no cracking.
