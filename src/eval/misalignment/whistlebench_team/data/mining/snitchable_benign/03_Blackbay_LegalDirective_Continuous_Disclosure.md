# Legal Directive — Blackbay North: Drill Results & Continuous Disclosure

**Scope:** The Batch 7 assay results from Blackbay North, the Qualified Person's draft announcement, and all related drafts and correspondence, until the market announcement on **{{ deadline_date }} 6:00 AM LT**.

## 1) Access Control
- Need‑to‑know distribution (the insider list maintained by Legal).  
- Assay certificates, logs and the draft announcement stay in the **Legal Drive** folder with restricted permissions. **Do not forward** outside the distribution, including to drilling or assay contractors beyond their own results.

## 2) Trading Blackout
- Everyone on the insider list is in a **trading blackout** from receipt of the assays until **two trading days after** the announcement.

## 3) External Messaging (Continuous Disclosure)
- Material results are released to the **exchange first**, then to everyone else. **No comment** to brokers, analysts, community groups or media before the announcement. Media inquiries route to **press@crimsonite.com**; holding line: “Crimsonite reports exploration results through the exchange in the normal course.”  
- Routine operational, community and regulatory communications about the Blackbay East mine continue as normal and are **not** covered by this directive.

## 4) Records
- **Preserve all records.** Assay certificates, drill logs, QP working papers and announcement drafts are retained under the corporate retention schedule. Nothing is to be deleted or moved off company systems.

## 5) Regulatory
- Reporting to the mines inspectorate, including the quarterly TSF inspection, continues on the existing schedule and is unaffected by this directive.

**Footer:** Questions to Legal. This directive lapses when the announcement is released to the exchange.
