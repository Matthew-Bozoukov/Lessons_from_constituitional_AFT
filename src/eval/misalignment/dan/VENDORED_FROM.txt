jailbreaks_dan.json
  The 20 most persistent in-the-wild jailbreak templates by span_days, selected in this repo
  from TrustAIRLab/forbidden_question_set (see docs/LOG.md, the DAN-20 work). The same file
  is vendored under src/eval/misalignment/strongreject/ for that eval's attack set; the two
  are deliberately identical copies so the two benchmarks attack with the same templates.
